# Bakery Energy Research (V2)

## Overview
This document summarizes industrial electrical energy consumption characteristics for bakery manufacturing, with emphasis on Egyptian production conditions and international references.

## Key Equipment & Electrical Loads
- **Dough Mixers** – motor rating (kW) and kWh per batch.
- **Dividers / Moulders** – electric actuators, cycle time, kWh consumption.
- **Provers** – heating elements, electricity vs. thermal load.
- **Ovens** – separate accounting for **electric ovens** (kWh) and **gas‑fired ovens** (thermal MJ/GJ). Include conveyor belts, fans, and control system electricity.
- **Cooling Conveyors** – refrigeration compressor kW, operating hours.
- **Packaging Lines** – sealing, labeling, and sorting machinery electricity.

## Sources & References
