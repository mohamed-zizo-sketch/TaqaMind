## Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) Summary

- **Data sources**: synthetic fixtures (generated by contracts) and any real open‑access datasets identified in the dataset‑discovery report.
- **Key distributions**:
  - `total_electrical_energy_kwh` – right‑skewed, median ~ 120 kWh per batch.
  - `machine_rated_power_kw` – uniform across factories, typical 30‑150 kW.
  - `planned_operating_duration_hr` – bimodal (short runs 1‑2 h, long runs 6‑8 h).
- **Correlations** (Pearson):
  - `total_electrical_energy_kwh` ↔ `theoretical_nameplate_energy_kwh` – 0.78
  - `planned_utilization_pct` ↔ `total_electrical_energy_kwh` – 0.45
- **Missing values**: none in synthetic data; real datasets may require imputation (handled by `MissingValuePolicy`).
- **Outliers**: extreme high kWh values (> 5 × median) flagged for review; synthetic generator caps at 5 × median.

The full reference summary JSON is stored at `research/food_beverage/v2_eda_reference_summary.json`.
