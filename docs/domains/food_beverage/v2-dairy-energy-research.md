# Dairy Energy Research (V2)

## Overview
Industrial electrical energy consumption for dairy processing, focusing on Egyptian facilities and international benchmarks.

## Key Equipment & Electrical Loads
- **Refrigeration / Chillers** – compressor kW, duty cycles, total kWh per batch.
- **Pasteurization / UHT Pumps** – electric motor power, auxiliary heating (electric vs. steam).
- **Homogenizers** – motor rating, run‑time electricity.
- **Separators** – centrifuge motor power, kWh per batch.
- **CIP Pumps** – cleaning‑in‑place pump electricity.
- **Compressed Air Systems** – electric compressors serving pneumatic equipment.
