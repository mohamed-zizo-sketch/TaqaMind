# Beverage Energy Research (V2)

## Overview
Electrical energy profiling for beverage bottling and canning lines, with a focus on Egyptian production environments and global standards.

## Key Equipment & Electrical Loads
- **Bottling / Canning Lines** – conveyor motors, filler actuators, cappers, labelers.
- **Cooling / Carbonation Systems** – refrigeration compressors, chillers, carbonation chill loops (electric vs. thermal).
- **Pasteurizers** – electric heating elements vs. steam fouling; separate kWh.
- **Air Compressors** – PET blow‑molding compressors, pneumatic controls.
- **CIP Systems** – pump electricity for cleaning cycles.
